# Tonight Action Plan

## First 2 Hours Of Launch Prep

### 0-15 Minutes: Confirm The Site Works

- Open https://sofa2slugger.com/
- Confirm homepage loads.
- Confirm Round 1 opens.
- Confirm the intro audio player appears.
- Confirm the full course page opens.
- Confirm payment button appears, without running a live payment test unless planned.
- Check the site on mobile.
- Check the site on desktop.

### 15-30 Minutes: Collect Screenshots

- Capture homepage hero.
- Capture the "Why this exists" section.
- Capture the intro audio area.
- Capture the round/course list.
- Capture Round 1 page.
- Capture mobile homepage.
- Store screenshots in one folder for launch use.

### 30-40 Minutes: Choose First X Post

- Pick one short direct post from `x-posts.md`.
- Keep the link visible.
- Do not over-explain.
- Post once.
- Log the post URL.

### 40-70 Minutes: Find 10 Reddit Communities

- Search for beginner fitness communities.
- Search for home workout communities.
- Search for no-equipment exercise communities.
- Search for beginner boxing or martial arts question communities.
- Search for habit-building communities.
- Read rules before posting anywhere.
- Note which communities allow self-promotion, feedback requests, or weekly promo threads.
- Add 10 candidates to a simple notes file.

### 70-100 Minutes: Send 10 Private Feedback Requests

- Choose 10 people who may give honest feedback.
- Use messages from `feedback.md` or `outreach-messages.md`.
- Ask for Round 1 feedback.
- Do not chase instant replies.
- Log who was contacted.

### 100-120 Minutes: Log Results

- Record X post URL.
- Record Reddit communities researched.
- Record people contacted.
- Record replies received.
- Record confusing objections.
- Record any bugs or trust issues.
- Decide the next single action for tomorrow.
