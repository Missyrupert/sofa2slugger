# Stranger Feedback Script

## Short Message

Hi. I made a private audio-guided beginner boxing course called Sofa2Slugger.

It is for people who want to move again at home without a gym, bag, audience, or fitness-bro nonsense. Round 1 is free.

Would you be willing to try Round 1 and give me blunt feedback? I am mostly trying to learn what is clear, what feels trustworthy, and what puts people off.

https://sofa2slugger.com/

## Feedback Questions

1. Before clicking anything, what did you think Sofa2Slugger was?
2. Did the line "No gym. No bag. No audience." make sense to you?
3. Did the homepage feel trustworthy? Why or why not?
4. Did anything feel confusing, exaggerated, or off-putting?
5. Did you understand that it is audio-guided rather than video-led?
6. Did you understand who it is for and who it is not for?
7. Did Round 1 feel approachable for a beginner?
8. Did anything make you worry about safety, embarrassment, or difficulty?
9. Would you share this with anyone? If yes, who?
10. Would you consider paying for the full course? Why or why not?
