# Positioning

## One-Line Positioning

Sofa2Slugger is a private audio-guided beginner boxing course for normal people who want a way back into movement without a gym, bag, audience, or fitness-bro nonsense.

## Who It Is For

- People who want to move again but do not feel like fitness people.
- Beginners who want to learn basic boxing movement privately.
- People who feel awkward starting in a gym.
- People who want short, structured movement at home.
- People who prefer clear instruction over hype.
- People who want to build rhythm, balance, guard, and confidence slowly.

## Who It Is Not For

- Serious boxers.
- People looking for hardcore fight training.
- People who want video-led workouts.
- People who want calorie tracking, leaderboards, or influencer-style fitness content.
- People looking for medical advice, professional coaching, or fight preparation.

## Core Promise

Sofa2Slugger gives beginners a private, structured way to stand up, learn basic shadowboxing movement, and build confidence at home without needing equipment, an audience, or a fitness identity.

## Core Objections

- "I am too unfit for boxing."
- "I do not want to go to a gym."
- "I do not have a bag or equipment."
- "I will feel stupid doing this."
- "I do not want to watch workout videos."
- "I do not want hype or fake motivation."
- "I am not sure if this is safe for beginners."
- "I do not want a subscription."

## Short Product Description

Sofa2Slugger is a private audio-guided beginner boxing course that teaches basic stance, guard, punches, rhythm, movement, and shadowboxing at home. No gym, no bag, no audience.

## Longer Product Description

Sofa2Slugger is a 12-round audio-guided beginner boxing course for normal people who want a way back into movement without joining a gym, buying equipment, filming themselves, or pretending they already know what they are doing.

The course uses boxing as a simple structure for movement: stance, guard, straight punches, rhythm, defense, footwork, and a first full shadowboxing round. Audio is intentional. The user listens, moves, and stops staring at a screen.

It is not professional boxing coaching, fight training, medical advice, or a performance lifestyle product. It is a calm, private, low-pressure way to start moving again.
