# Sofa2Slugger: Short-Form Video (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) Scripts

This document contains **5 custom-written short-form video scripts** designed to promote **Sofa2Slugger**. Each script is structured for **seamless looping** (where the ending sentence connects directly back to the beginning hook), which significantly boosts watch-time and algorithm recommendation rates.

---

## 🎧 Setup & Voice Generation
To generate the voiceovers with your custom voice clone, use the helper script:
```bash
python generate_shorts_voiceovers.py --short <1-5>
```
This will automatically parse your `.env.local` file for your `ELEVENLABS_COACH_VOICE_ID` and save the output as `short_<1-5>_voiceover.mp3`.

*   **Theme:** Small habits create massive momentum.
*   **Visual Suggestions:**
    *   **0:00–0:09:** A boxer throwing fast, light jabs (out of focus or silhouette).
    *   **0:09–0:22:** A person putting on athletic shoes or stepping out their door early in the morning.
    *   **0:22–0:30:** A close-up of a laptop, phone, or notebook showing a small step done.
    *   **0:30–End:** The Sofa2Slugger app screen ticking down on the floor.
*   **Loop Mechanism:** The end words *"which is why..."* flow directly into the start *"Most people who want to learn boxing..."*
