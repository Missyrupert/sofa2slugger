# Sofa2Slugger: Short-Form Video Assets Wishlist

To make your short-form videos look incredibly premium, atmospheric, and highly engaging, we need to move beyond generic stock footage. The most viral videos on TikTok, Shorts, and Reels are **raw, authentic, and cinematic, filmed on a mobile phone in a real room.**

Here is the master checklist of visual and audio assets we should prepare or film to match our 5 new scripts.

---

## 📸 1. Visual B-Roll Loops (9:16 Vertical Video)
You can film all of these in **10 minutes in your own living room or training space** using a phone and a basic tripod or placing your phone against a wall/furniture. 
*   **Lighting:** Warm and low-key (e.g., golden-hour sunlight through a window, or a single warm desk lamp in a dark room). Let the background fall into shadows.
*   **Settings:** Set your phone camera to **4K at 60 FPS** (or 24 FPS for a cinematic look) and turn on "Grid lines" to keep things centered.

### The Shot List:
- [ ] **Shot A: The Earbud Insertion (The Hook)**
  *   *Action:* Close-up (side profile) of you putting a wireless earbud (like AirPods or similar) into your ear.
  *   *Why it's used:* Syncs perfectly with our "noise-cancellation" audio hook in the first 3 seconds.
- [ ] **Shot B: The Hand Wrap (Atmosphere)**
  *   *Action:* Slow, close-up shot of black boxing hand wraps being wound around your knuckles.
  *   *Why it's used:* Shows preparation, dedication, and grit.
- [ ] **Shot C: Stance Footwork (Instructional/Rhythm)**
  *   *Action:* Camera placed low on the floor, filming your feet on a wooden floor or rug. Step back and pivot into your Orthodox/Southpaw stance, then rock back and forth.
  *   *Why it's used:* Visualizes the foundation of boxing and matches the rhythm of the scripts.
- [ ] **Shot D: The Silhouette Shadowboxer (Action)**
  *   *Action:* Phone placed on a tripod filming your whole body shadowboxing. Position yourself in front of a light source (like a window or lamp) so you appear as a dark silhouette throwing clean jabs and slips.
  *   *Why it's used:* Very atmospheric, high-contrast, and lets the viewer project themselves into the video.
- [ ] **Shot E: Tying the Shoes (Grit)**
  *   *Action:* Close-up of you tying the laces on your training shoes or boxing boots.
  *   *Why it's used:* Establishes a sense of starting a workout.

## 🎵 3. Sound Design & Music Assets
Audiences interact with short-form video through sound. Having crisp, high-impact audio assets is 80% of the game.

- [ ] **Asset A: The "Whoosh" / "Suck" Sound Effect (SFX)**
  *   *Use:* Plays at 0:03 when the ambient noise cuts out and the user puts their earbud in (representing active noise-cancellation).
- [ ] **Asset B: The "Click" SFX**
  *   *Use:* A satisfying tactile click sound that plays exactly when the earbud is tapped.
- [ ] **Asset C: High-Energy Boxing Bell**
  *   *Use:* The clean, metallic ring of a round bell starting a round. (We have `round-bell.wav` in the folder, which is perfect!).
- [ ] **Asset D: Music Beds (Lofi / Synthwave / Grime)**
  *   *Use:* The background music that fades in after the bell. It should be instrumental, rhythmic, and loopable. (We have `promo_music.wav`, but having 2-3 different beats will prevent our videos from sounding repetitive).
