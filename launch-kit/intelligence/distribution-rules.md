# Distribution Rules

- No spam.
- No arguing.
- No fake urgency.
- No pretending to be a coach.
- No mass DMing.
- No auto-posting.
- No heroics.
- Useful first, product second.
- Emotional alignment over traffic volume.

## Practical Meaning

If the product is not relevant, do not mention it.

If a thread is hostile, leave it alone.

If someone needs medical, injury, mental health, or professional boxing advice, do not try to make Sofa2Slugger the answer.

If the best reply is just a helpful sentence with no link, use that.

If a product mention feels even slightly shoehorned, it probably is.
