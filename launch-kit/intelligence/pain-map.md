# Pain Map

## Gym Anxiety

Emotional description:
The person wants to start moving but feels exposed, watched, or out of place in a gym.

Likely user language:
- "I'm too embarrassed to go to the gym."
- "I feel like everyone will judge me."
- "I don't know what I'm doing in there."
- "The gym feels intimidating."

Likely conversion strength: 5

Recommended reply tone:
Calm, validating, practical. Emphasise private first steps.
