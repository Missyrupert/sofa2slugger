# X Research Workflow

Use X to find live conversations, not to force replies into places they do not belong.

No auto-posting. No mass replies. No fake enthusiasm.

## Search Phrase: "too embarrassed to go to the gym"

Look for:
- People describing gym anxiety.
- People asking for private alternatives.
- People who sound open to advice.

Reply when:
- They ask for ideas.
- The thread is calm.
- A short private-start suggestion would help.

Do not reply when:
- They are venting and not asking.
- The thread is hostile.
- A product mention would feel like a drive-by.

Soft reply example:
Starting somewhere private is valid. A gym can come later. Ten quiet minutes at home is less glamorous, but it counts.

## Search Phrase: "want to start boxing"

Look for:
- Beginners asking how to start.
- People nervous about boxing gyms.
- People asking about no-equipment basics.

Reply when:
- They ask what first steps make sense.
- They mention being intimidated.

Do not reply when:
- They want fight coaching.
- They are asking about sparring or injury.

Soft reply example:
Start with stance, guard, and simple shadowboxing before worrying about bags or gyms. The first step does not need to be dramatic.

## Search Phrase: "gym anxiety"

Look for:
- People who avoid gyms because they feel watched.
- People asking for low-pressure starts.

Reply when:
- They want practical suggestions.
- You can help without diagnosing anything.

Do not reply when:
- It is a serious mental health thread.
- The conversation is about therapy or medication.

Soft reply example:
A private start can be a good bridge. Home, headphones, short session, done. No audience required.

## Search Phrase: "need to get fit but"

Look for:
- People naming a specific blocker.
- People overwhelmed by the idea of starting.

Reply when:
- The blocker is time, embarrassment, equipment, or decision fatigue.

Do not reply when:
- The blocker is medical.
- They are asking for weight-loss guarantees.

Soft reply example:
Make the first step small enough that it cannot become a whole project. Five or ten minutes, repeatable, no grand declaration.

## Search Phrase: "I keep procrastinating"

Look for:
- People stuck before starting.
- People asking for tiny habit ideas.

Reply when:
- They want a practical nudge.

Do not reply when:
- The thread is unrelated to movement.
- It is just a joke thread.

Soft reply example:
Remove choices. Pick one tiny thing with a clear end point. Starting is easier when the task is too small to negotiate with.

## Search Phrase: "home workout beginner"

Look for:
- People asking for beginner-friendly home options.
- People who do not have equipment.

Reply when:
- They ask for specific formats.

Do not reply when:
- They need a full custom training plan.
- The thread is already full of better expert answers.

Soft reply example:
For home starts, keep it simple: clear space, short session, basic movement, stop before it turns into a punishment ritual.

## Search Phrase: "hate fitness influencers"

Look for:
- People rejecting performative fitness.
- People asking for less annoying alternatives.

Reply when:
- A plain, non-hype suggestion fits.

Do not reply when:
- It is just dunking.
- It is becoming personal or mean.

Soft reply example:
Look for instruction that does not need you to admire the instructor. Boring, clear, repeatable is underrated.

## Search Phrase: "don't know where to start fitness"

Look for:
- People overwhelmed by options.
- People asking for first steps.

Reply when:
- You can suggest a small repeatable start.

Do not reply when:
- They ask for medical, diet, or injury advice.

Soft reply example:
Start with the smallest structured thing you will actually repeat. Walk, bodyweight basics, shadowboxing, whatever. The format matters less than removing friction.

## Search Phrase: "shadow boxing at home"

Look for:
- People asking if it is useful.
- People asking how to start without a bag.

Reply when:
- The question is beginner-level.

Do not reply when:
- They are asking about fight training.
- They need technical coaching beyond basics.

Soft reply example:
Shadowboxing at home is useful for basics: stance, guard, rhythm, and balance. Just keep it light and controlled. Your furniture has done nothing wrong.

## Search Phrase: "no motivation to exercise"

Look for:
- People asking how to start.
- People who dislike hype.

Reply when:
- They want a practical first step.

Do not reply when:
- It is a medical or crisis thread.
- A reply would feel like a lecture.

Soft reply example:
Do not wait for motivation. Make the first action smaller. Stand up, move for five minutes, stop. Repeatability beats intensity at the start.
