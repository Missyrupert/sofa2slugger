# Automation Roadmap

Principle: Automate research, scoring, logging, and drafting. Do not automate public posting yet.

## Level 1: Manual-Assisted

- ChatGPT/Codex finds relevant conversations.
- ChatGPT/Codex scores fit and spam risk.
- ChatGPT/Codex drafts reply options.
- Chris reviews everything.
- Chris posts manually, or chooses not to post.
- Results are logged in Markdown.

Best for:
- Early launch.
- Learning which communities are receptive.
- Avoiding tone mistakes.
- Keeping the human judgment intact.

## Level 2: Semi-Automated

- Maintain daily saved searches.
- Keep a CSV or Markdown lead log.
- Collect candidate threads and posts.
- Draft replies in batches.
- Review PostHog performance weekly.
- Track which replies lead to visits, Round 1 starts, and audio plays.
- Keep a list of communities where product mentions are not welcome.

Best for:
- Building a repeatable daily rhythm.
- Comparing channels.
- Finding patterns without acting weird online.

## Level 3: Advanced

- API-based monitoring where platform rules allow it.
- Scheduled daily or weekly opportunity reports.
- CRM-style outreach log.
- Community-level scoring.
- Reply outcome tracking.
- PostHog-assisted funnel review.
- Never auto-post without human approval.

Best for:
- Mature distribution work.
- Scaling research without scaling spam.
- Keeping a clear record of what worked and what should be left alone.
