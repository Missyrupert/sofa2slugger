# Sofa2Slugger Brand Kit

## Core Idea

Sofa2Slugger is private audio boxing for beginners. It turns an ordinary room into a first round without asking the user to perform, film themselves, join a gym, or watch another trainer on a screen.

## Brand Feel

- Cinematic living-room boxing
- Warm, physical, rhythmic, and direct
- Beginner-aware without looking soft
- Anti-doomscrolling, but not preachy
- More private fight-camp than fitness influencer

## Logo Direction

Use a strong uppercase `SOFA 2 SLUGGER` wordmark. The `2` should feel like a round card or earned badge, not a red button. It can be slightly angled and tactile, with brass and cream replacing red.

Avoid literal ring-girl imagery for now. The round-card idea is useful, but the brand should feel inclusive and premium rather than tacky or fight-night cliché.

## Image Direction

The hero image remains central: someone stuck on the sofa, phone glowing, the first round waiting. Images should feel cinematic, low-lit, human, and grounded. Avoid glossy gyms, fake sweat, stock fitness smiles, and cartoon boxing graphics.

## UI Rules

- Audio is the product; screens support the act of listening and moving.
- Buttons should feel clear and physical.
- Cards should be simple and sharp, not decorative.
- Keep the visual world warm, dark, and tactile.
- Safety content should be clear, plain, and impossible to misunderstand.

## Tone Of Voice

Short, grounded, and encouraging.

Say:
- "Less watching. More moving."
- "Start where you are."
- "Pause, reset, go again."
- "Round 1 is free."

Avoid:
- Hype-bro fitness language
- Overpromising transformation
- Boxing machismo
- Medical claims
- Anything that makes beginners feel exposed
